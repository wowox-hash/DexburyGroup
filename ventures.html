Questions founders ask first.

Anything else? Ask us directly; we reply within 2 working days.

Do you invest?

Yes, in selected projects, through minority stakes and usually alongside our advisory support. Amounts depend on the project and are decided case by case.

Which sectors do you look at?

Any industry, product or service where brand, channels or innovation can create value.

Can I get advisory without investment?

Yes. Advisory can stand alone, paid or in a mix agreed with you.

What happens after I submit?

We read every submission personally and reply to each one. If there is a fit, we propose a first meeting.
