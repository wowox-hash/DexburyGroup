Frédéric Decroix, founder.

Frédéric is a senior marketing and commercial leader. He has led global marketing, brand management and partnerships for international lubricant brands sold in more than 100 markets, and now leads trade channel management with international buying groups and e-commerce development in the automotive aftermarket.

Earlier in his career, in the tyre and automotive services sector, he managed portfolios of product and service brands, including a B2C service network.

His expertise spans global marketing strategy, brand building, partnerships and sponsorship, motorsport marketing, B2B distribution and international team leadership.
